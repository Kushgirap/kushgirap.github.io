01 / Win probability model · Live

A live win probability model for the Big Bash League. Set the match state and watch where a chase falls apart.

A random train/test split reports 78% accuracy; the honest one reports 73%. A third experiment showed half the gap is leakage and half is the cost of predicting future seasons. Trained on 647 BBL matches.

02 / Sheffield Shield season analysis 2025/26

Three Tableau dashboards reading a whole Sheffield Shield season back to the analysts who cover the team.

Every South Australian win came batting second; batting first, they won none of four. Built on an end-to-end Python pipeline and shared with a performance analyst at SACA.

03 / Post-match coaching report · Sixers vs Thunder

I coded every ball of one BBL match by hand to find out why Thunder lost.

Smith struck at 341 against right-arm pace and at 100 against Sangha's leg spin. Three tactical findings from 239 hand-coded deliveries, written for a coach rather than an analyst.
