02 / Sheffield Shield season analysis 2025/26

Three Tableau dashboards reading a whole Sheffield Shield season back to the analysts who cover the team.

An end-to-end pipeline and three dashboards covering South Australia's season, built as a pre-final briefing.

The batting insights dashboard, one of three in the briefing.

The question

South Australia had ten group-stage matches behind them going into the Sheffield Shield final. What did that season actually say about how they bat and bowl?

What it covers

Three Tableau dashboards built as a pre-final briefing: a season overview, batting insights, and bowling insights.

  • Batting. Partnership quality and dismissal patterns, shown alongside the headline averages.
  • Bowling. Phase-level bowling analysis, not just economy and wickets.

Shared with a performance analyst at SACA ahead of the final.

How it was built

  • Pipeline. An end-to-end Python pipeline parses Cricsheet ball-by-ball JSON into structured delivery-level records.
  • Features. Engineered fields for phase of innings, partnership identifiers, and collapse detection.
  • Dashboards. The processed data feeds the three Tableau dashboards.
